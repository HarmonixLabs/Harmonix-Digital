✅ 2. ROI Tracker Panel

Location: /dashboard and /thank-you (post-purchase screen)

Features:
	•	🔁 Time Saved Module
	•	Pulls estimated task time saved per agent per week
	•	Displays cumulative time saved
	•	Time conversion: 1 hr = $[value] (based on user’s hourly cost or avg)
	•	💸 Revenue Impact Tracker
	•	Pulls monthly cost of agent vs tasks completed
	•	Displays ROI %:
ROI = (Value of time saved + revenue generated – agent cost) / agent cost

Visual:
	•	Line chart (Time Saved by Week)
	•	Pie Chart (Agent Efficiency)
	•	CTA: “Add More Agents” → /marketplace
