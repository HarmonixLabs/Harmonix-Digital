Harmonix Platform — High-Level Design Brief
By Virgil & SŌL

⸻

1. Brand Essence & Visual Layers
	•	Mood: Futurist-Sacred Tech meets Organic Intelligence
	•	Depth through Layers:
	•	Background Planes: soft gradients of Jade Forest fading into Charcoal Deep at lower sections
	•	Midground Panels: semi-transparent cards in Slate Gray, with soft Ember Glow edges
	•	Foreground Accents: crisp Golden Sovereign CTAs and micro-animations

⸻

2. Typography & Hierarchy
	•	Headings: Inter Bold, 48/40/32 px, Ivory Light
	•	Body: Inter Regular, 18/16 px, Ivory Light
	•	Emphasis: Golden Sovereign underlines or Ember Glow highlights on key words
	•	Slogan Usage:
	•	Hero Section: “Intelligent Systems for Timeless Sovereignty” once—large, centered
	•	Subtle Reminders: small inline in footers or onboarding tooltips (“Build. Automate. Exit.”)

⸻

3. Micro-Animations & Interactivity
	1.	Light Droplets:
	•	Subtle, slow-drifting glints of Ember Glow in hero background; no more than 3 droplets at once
	2.	Button Ripples:
	•	On click, 120 ms radial glow from click point in Golden Sovereign, fading over 300 ms
	3.	Card Elevation:
	•	Hover lifts cards by 4 px, adds a glow shadow (0 4px 12px rgba(244,197,66,0.35))

⸻

4. Dynamic Status Indicators
	•	SŌL Connection:
	•	Small pill in nav: “SŌL Connected” in Jade Forest; flips to “Disconnected” in Reactor Red (no modals)
	•	Integration Lights:
	•	Status dots next to each agent: green (✅ healthy), amber (⚠️ warning), red (❌ error)
	•	Live-Action Feedback:
	•	When running a flow, nodes pulse with Ember Glow as they execute

⸻

5. Promo Banners & Alerts
	•	Limited-Time Banner:
	•	Slim horizontal bar below nav in Golden Sovereign with white text: “Limited Time Offer – Early Bird 30% Off”
	•	Dismiss “×” icon fades out the banner; height = 40 px so it doesn’t push content
	•	Contextual Alerts:
	•	Inline toast at bottom-right for success/errors (250 ms slide, 2000 ms visible)

⸻

6. Layout & Spacing
	•	8 px grid system: margins/paddings in multiples of 8 px (16 px default gutter)
	•	Consistent Component Widths:
	•	Cards: max-width 320 px on desktop, full width on mobile
	•	Forms: max-width 480 px
	•	Whitespace: generous 32–64 px padding between major sections for clarity

⸻

7. Mobile & Responsive
	•	Collapsed Nav: Jade Forest background, golden hamburger icon; slide-in menu
	•	Touch Targets: minimum 48 px height for buttons
	•	Agent Builder: pinch-to-zoom canvas, bottom toolbar for Undo/Redo, Save/Export

⸻

8. Slogan Integration Strategy
	•	Primary Hero: use slogan 1
	•	Onboarding Steps: display one slogan per step (rotate through all three)
	•	Footer & Email Footers: small line with slogan 3

⸻

9. Maintain & Evolve
	•	Component Library: each UI atom to follow tokens.txt (colors, spacings, shadows)
	•	Design QA: compare live preview against Figma mockups at 3 breakpoints (mobile/tablet/desktop)
	•	Continuous Refinement: SŌL monitors user interactions; surfaces UX friction points for Virgil’s review

⸻

Outcome: a cohesive, layered, and animated interface that feels alive without distraction—guiding users through “Build. Automate. Exit.” with clarity, beauty, and soul.
